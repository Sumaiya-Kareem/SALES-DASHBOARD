# 📊 Sales Dashboard Project

## 📌 Project Description
This project presents an interactive **Sales Dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize sales performance across multiple dimensions such as geography, sales channels, and customer demographics. It enables stakeholders to quickly identify trends, evaluate business performance, and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard creation  
- **SQL (PostgreSQL/MySQL)** – Data extraction and transformation  
- **Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing  
- **Python (basic scripts)** – Data manipulation and automation  

---

## 📂 Data Source
- **Sales transaction dataset** containing customer IDs, sales amounts, shipping cities/states, channels, and age groups.  
- Data was cleaned and structured using **Excel and SQL** before being imported into Power BI for visualization.

---

## ✨ Features
- **KPIs Overview**: Total sales amount and customer count  
- **Geographical Insights**: Sales distribution by city and state  
- **Channel Analysis**: Performance comparison across Amazon, Flipkart, eBay, Meesho, Snapdeal, and others  
- **Demographic Insights**: Sales segmented by age groups (Adult, Teenager, Senior)  
- **Time Filter**: Monthly selection for trend analysis  

---

## 💡 Example Business Problems Addressed
- Which **sales channel** contributes the highest revenue?  
- Which **state or city** drives the most sales?  
- How do **different age groups** impact overall sales performance?  
- What are the **seasonal trends** in monthly sales?  

---

## 📈 Key Insights
- **Amazon** dominates with **7.5M** in sales, followed by Flipkart and eBay.  
- **Uttar Pradesh** leads state-wise sales with **2.3M**, highlighting regional demand concentration.  
- **Adults** contribute the largest share (**50.1%**) of revenue, followed by teenagers and seniors.  
- **Tirupati city** shows the highest individual city sales, indicating potential for targeted marketing.
